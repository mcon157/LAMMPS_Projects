\textbf{Take-aways.} (1) $\Delta H_f(\mathrm{WC})$ and all $\mu$-independent combinations agree with experiment/DFT. (2) The potential puts W2C far above WC + W, so the W-rich limit is bcc W, and a C-depleted layer would separate into WC + W rather than form W2C. (3) $V_\mathrm C$ is the dominant defect (0.5--0.9\,eV), so WC is always slightly C-poor; C vacancies repel each other, so they stay dispersed. (4) $C_i$ sits in the same channel site as He.

\subsection*{Limitations}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Transferability.} W--He and C--He pair potentials were not fitted in WC. Error unknown, possibly tenths of an eV. $\to$ DFT on $\sim$12--15 small cells ($\le2$ vacancies, $\le4$ He).
\item \textbf{Host potential.} ABOP vacancy and divacancy energetics in WC unchecked; controls the sign of $\Eb(V{+}V)$. $\to$ compare $V_\mathrm{W}$, $V_\mathrm{C}$, $V_\mathrm{W}V_\mathrm{C}$ with DFT.
\item \textbf{0\,K statics.} No vibrational entropy, no zero-point energy. Matters for 0.01--0.1\,eV bonds (He--He), barely for eV-scale trapping.
\item \textbf{Search.} Stochastic; can miss the global minimum in crowded clusters. Shows as \cmd{TRIALS-DIFFER} / \cmd{FOUND-ONCE}; v3: $\pm$0.1\,eV in the most crowded rows.
\item \textbf{Cluster geometry.} One vacancy arrangement per cluster size; only the He were searched. Other arrangements (mixed clusters) may bind more.
\item \textbf{Cell size.} One 1280-atom cell; image strain grows with cluster size. $\to$ repeat key rows at $10\times6\times10$.
\item \textbf{Chemical potential.} Vacancy \emph{formation} energies need $\mu_\mathrm{W},\mu_\mathrm{C}$; every \emph{binding} energy here is independent of them.
\item \textbf{No kinetics.} No barriers, so no detrapping energy $E_\mathrm{dt}=\Eb+E_\mathrm{m}$ or desorption temperature. $\to$ NEB.
\item \textbf{Version.} Decks follow the 29 Sep 2021 compute-currency rules; newer LAMMPS is more permissive, so they still run.
\item \textbf{Formation energies.} One W2C structure; graphite = graphene (no interlayer term); dilute defects, no formation entropy ($g=1$); equilibrium only -- a sputter-depleted surface is outside it.
\end{itemize}
\end{multicols}

\subsection{Binding energies (\texttt{he\_be\_v3})}
\textbf{Run order:} \cmd{make\_v3.py} (already run) $\rightarrow$ \cmd{submit\_v3.sh}: \cmd{in.bulk0}
$\rightarrow$ (\cmd{in.ref} and the eight \cmd{in.<series>} in parallel) $\rightarrow$
\cmd{analyze\_v3.py} $\rightarrow$ \cmd{plot\_v3.py}, \cmd{check\_he\_positions.py}.

\begin{multicols}{2}
\small
\textbf{\cmd{make\_v3.py}}
\begin{enumerate}
\item List the perfect-lattice W, C and channel sites of the $8\times5\times8$ cell in cell units.
\item Build each cluster: seed site W0 = (4.5, 2.5, 4) or C0 = (4.5, 3.167, 4.5); add one site at a time (compact rule; \cmd{vw2a} restricted to the basal plane).
\item For each series and $n$: TRIALS random He placements inside the void (one for $n=0$); centre and radius of the core sphere.
\item Write each cfg: \cmd{variable n m trial}; a \cmd{region sphere} per vacated site, \cmd{group vac}, \cmd{nvac}, \cmd{delete\_atoms}; \cmd{region core}; one \cmd{create\_atoms 3 single} per He $\rightarrow$ \wfile{cfg/<series>\_<k>.lmp}, \wfile{cfg/index.txt}.
\item Fill the deck template (setup, record, 3$\times$ anneal + record, loop tail) $\rightarrow$ \wfile{in.<series>}, \wfile{in.ref}, \wfile{series.json}.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{\cmd{in.bulk0}}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \cmd{units metal}, \cmd{atom\_style atomic}, \cmd{boundary p p p}.
\item \cmd{lattice custom} (orthogonal WC basis, 614.6\,K guess for $a$, $c$); \cmd{region}/\cmd{create\_box 3}/\cmd{create\_atoms} (basis 1--2 W, 3--4 C); masses.
\item \cmd{pair\_style hybrid/overlay} + four \cmd{pair\_coeff}; neighbour list.
\item \cmd{fix box/relax ... couple xy} + CG; \cmd{unfix}; CG again.
\item Print $a$, $c$, $E_\mathrm{bulk}$ $\rightarrow$ \wfile{wc\_bulk0.restart}.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{\cmd{in.ref}}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \rfile{wc\_bulk0.restart}; pair lines; \cmd{run 0} $\rightarrow$ $E_\mathrm{bulk}$.
\item \cmd{create\_atoms 3 single} at the channel site (4.5, 2.833, 4.5); CG; \cmd{run 0}.
\item Print $E_\mathrm{bulk}$, $E(\He_1)$, $E_\mathrm{sol}$ $\rightarrow$ \wfile{res\_ref.dat}, \wfile{dump.ref.lammps}.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{\cmd{in.<series>}} (e.g.\ \cmd{in.vw1})
\begin{enumerate}
\item Once: \rfile{wc\_bulk0.restart}, pair lines, \cmd{run 0} $\rightarrow$ $E_\mathrm{bulk}$; header lines of \wfile{res\_<series>.dat}; \cmd{mkdir dumps}.
\item Loop over $k$: \cmd{clear}; \rfile{wc\_bulk0.restart}; pair, neighbour, \cmd{timestep 0.001}, \cmd{cg} + \cmd{line quadratic}.
\item \cmd{include} \rfile{cfg/<series>\_<k>.lmp}: delete the vacated atoms, define core, add the He.
\item Groups \cmd{host}, \cmd{heg}; $R_{g0}$; computes \cmd{dsp}, \cmd{dmax}, \cmd{nfar}; \cmd{thermo\_style} with them.
\item Cycle 1: CG $\rightarrow$ \cmd{run 0} $\rightarrow$ print \cmd{k n m trial nvac 1 E dmax nfar kin rg rg0} $\rightarrow$ dump.

\sect{Equations}
% =====================================================================
$E(X)$ = relaxed total energy of a cell containing defect $X$ (same box as $\Eblk$).
$\He_1$ = one interstitial He at the reference site. $X$ = vacancy cluster with $n_\mathrm{W}$ W and $n_\mathrm{C}$ C sites; $X'$ = its parent (one site fewer).

\subsection{WC (\texttt{analyze\_v3.py})}
\begin{align}
E_\mathrm{sol} &= E(\He_1) - \Eblk \\
\Eb(\He\to\He_nX) &= E(\He_{n-1}X) + E(\He_1) - E(\He_nX) - \Eblk \\
\Eb(V_s\to\He_nX) &= E(\He_nX') + E(V_s) - E(\He_nX) - \Eblk, \qquad E(V_s)=E(\text{empty vw1 or vc1}) \\
\Eb^\mathrm{tot}(\He_nX) &= n_\mathrm{W}E(V_\mathrm{W}) + n_\mathrm{C}E(V_\mathrm{C}) + nE(\He_1) - E(\He_nX) - (n_\mathrm{W}+n_\mathrm{C}+n-1)\Eblk \\
E_\mathrm{int}(d) &= E(\He\text{--}\He\ \text{pair at } d) + \Eblk - 2E(\He_1) \qquad (\text{positive = repulsive})
\end{align}
For $n=0$, $\Eb^\mathrm{tot}$ is the binding of the empty cluster against isolated vacancies (positive = vacancies attract).
Every expression has the same number of each species on both sides, so the chemical potentials cancel.

\textbf{Selection.} For each (cluster, $n$): $E = \min\{E_{t,c} : n_\mathrm{far}=0\}$ over trials $t$ and cycles $c$ (if none is intact, the overall minimum, flagged \cmd{ALL-MUTATED}).
\begin{align*}
\text{hits} &= \#\{(t,c): E_{t,c}-E < \text{TOL}\}, &
\text{spread} &= \max_t\big(\min_c E_{t,c}\big) - E, \\
\text{MUTATED-STATE-LOWER-BY} &= E - \min_{\text{all}} E_{t,c}\ (\text{if}>\text{TOL}), &
\text{ESCAPED} &: k_\mathrm{in} < n, \qquad \text{SPREAD}: R_g - R_{g0} > 1.5\,\text{\AA}.
\end{align*}

\subsection{Diagnostics computed in the decks}
\begin{align*}
d_i &= |\mathbf r_i - \mathbf r_i^{0}| \ (\text{host atom } i), &
d_\mathrm{max} &= \max_i d_i, &
n_\mathrm{far} &= \textstyle\sum_i [\,d_i > 1\,\text{\AA}\,], \\
R_g &= \Big(\tfrac1{n}\textstyle\sum_j |\mathbf r_j - \mathbf r_\mathrm{cm}|^2\Big)^{1/2}\ (\text{He}), &
k_\mathrm{in} &= \#\{\He \in \text{core}\}, &
r_\mathrm{core} &= \max_s|\mathbf s - \bar{\mathbf s}| + 2.2\,\text{\AA}.
\end{align*}

\subsection{Geometry (\texttt{make\_v3.py})}\label{sec:geom}
\begin{itemize}
\item Minimum image: $\Delta x \leftarrow \Delta x - L_x\,\mathrm{round}(\Delta x/L_x)$ (same for $y,z$); distances use it everywhere.
\item Cluster growth: the new site of species $s$ is the free lattice site minimising $\sum_{\mathbf p\in X'} |\mathbf r-\mathbf p|$ (compact; ties $\to$ lowest coordinates). For \cmd{vw2} this gives the c-axis neighbour (2.815\,\AA, since $c<a$); \cmd{vw2a} is restricted to the same basal plane (2.919\,\AA).
\item He placement: uniform in spheres of radius $R_\mathrm{site}$ around the vacated sites, accepted if every He--He $\ge1.5$\,\AA\ and He--host $\ge1.5$\,\AA; on failure the limits relax stepwise (He--He to 1.0, He--host to 1.2\,\AA, radius $+0.2$, $+0.4$, $+0.6$\,\AA).
\item Positions are written in lattice-cell units of the restart box, $x = x_\mathrm{lo} + f_x L_x/N_x$ ($N_x=8$), so they follow the relaxed cell exactly.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{bcc-W benchmark (JW 2013 conventions)}
\begin{align}
E_\mathrm f(V) &= E(V) - \tfrac{N-1}{N}\Eblk, \qquad E_\mathrm f(\mathrm{SIA}) = E(\mathrm{SIA}) - \tfrac{N+1}{N}\Eblk \\
E_\mathrm f(\He,X) &= E(\He X) - E(X) - \varepsilon_\He \quad(\varepsilon_\He = 0 \text{ isolated, or } -0.00538\text{ eV fcc He}) \\
E_\mathrm f(\He_nV_m) &= E - (N-m)\,\varepsilon_\mathrm W - n\,\varepsilon_\He \quad\text{(JW eq.~4: formation vs bulk W and fcc He)} \\
\Eb(A\to B) &= E_\mathrm f(A) + E_\mathrm f(B) - E_\mathrm f(AB) \quad\text{(JW eq.~5; identical to the total-energy form when atoms balance)} \\
E_\mathrm m &\approx E(\He \text{ held at tet--tet midpoint}) - E(\He_\mathrm{tet}), \qquad E_\mathrm{dt} = \Eb + E_\mathrm m
\end{align}
Vacancy-cluster rule (JW): relax $V_m$, compute per-atom energy, remove the W atom with the highest energy (ties $\to$ lowest ID), relax $\to V_{m+1}$.
Benchmark score: $\mathrm{MAE} = \frac1N\sum|E^\text{this}-E^\text{JW}|$ = 0.015\,eV ($N$=46), max 0.072\,eV.

\subsection{Formation energies (\texttt{analyze\_ef.py})}\label{sec:ef}
$\Delta E(d) = E(d) - E_\mathrm{bulk}$; $d_\mathrm W$, $d_\mathrm C$ = atoms \emph{added} (a vacancy has $-1$).
\begin{align}
E_f(d) &= \Delta E(d) - d_\mathrm W\,\mu_\mathrm W - d_\mathrm C\,\mu_\mathrm C, \qquad \mu_\mathrm W + \mu_\mathrm C = \varepsilon_\mathrm{WC} = E_\mathrm{bulk}/N_\mathrm{f.u.} \\
\mu_\mathrm C &\le \varepsilon_\mathrm C, \qquad \mu_\mathrm W \le \min\!\big(\varepsilon_\mathrm W,\ \varepsilon_\mathrm{W_2C}-\varepsilon_\mathrm{WC}\big)\ \ (\text{W2C term only if } \varepsilon_\mathrm{W_2C} < \varepsilon_\mathrm{WC}+\varepsilon_\mathrm W) \\
\Delta H_f(\mathrm{WC}) &= \varepsilon_\mathrm{WC} - \varepsilon_\mathrm W - \varepsilon_\mathrm C \quad(= \text{window width when bcc W sets the W-rich limit}) \\
E_S &= \Delta E(V_\mathrm W) + \Delta E(V_\mathrm C) + \varepsilon_\mathrm{WC}, \quad E_{FP}^\mathrm C = \Delta E(V_\mathrm C) + \Delta E(C_i), \quad \Eb(X{+}Y) = E_f(X) + E_f(Y) - E_f(XY) \\
c_d &= g_d\,\frac{e^{-E_f/k_BT}}{1+e^{-E_f/k_BT}}, \qquad
\frac{N_\mathrm C}{N_\mathrm W} = \frac{1 - c_{V_\mathrm C} + c_{C_i} - c_{W_\mathrm C} + c_{C_\mathrm W} - 2c_{V_\mathrm C^2} - c_{V_\mathrm WV_\mathrm C}}{1 - c_{V_\mathrm W} + c_{W_i} + c_{W_\mathrm C} - c_{C_\mathrm W} - c_{V_\mathrm WV_\mathrm C}} \\
\text{50/50:}\ \ \frac{N_\mathrm C}{N_\mathrm W}(\mu_\mathrm C, T) &= 1, \qquad \text{WC}_{1-y}:\ \ \frac{N_\mathrm C}{N_\mathrm W}(\mu_\mathrm C, T) = 1-y \quad(\text{bisection; } N_\mathrm C/N_\mathrm W \text{ rises with } \mu_\mathrm C) \\
E_f(\He_nX) &= E(\He_nX) - E_\mathrm{bulk} + n_\mathrm W\mu_\mathrm W + n_\mathrm C\mu_\mathrm C \quad(\mu_\He = 0:\ \text{He from vacuum})
\end{align}

\subsection{Dynamics used by the search}
\begin{align*}
\text{Berendsen:}\quad \lambda &= \Big[1 + \tfrac{\Delta t}{\tau}\Big(\tfrac{T_\mathrm{target}}{T} - 1\Big)\Big]^{1/2},\ \mathbf v\leftarrow\lambda\mathbf v; &
\text{quench:}\quad T_\mathrm{target}(t) &= T + (1 - T)\,t/t_\mathrm{run}; \\
T &= \frac{2E_\mathrm{kin}}{(3N-3)\,k_\mathrm{B}}; &
\text{CG stop:}\quad \|\mathbf F\|_2 &= \Big(\textstyle\sum_{i=1}^{N}|\mathbf F_i|^2\Big)^{1/2} < \text{ftol}.
\end{align*}

\sect{Glossary}
% =====================================================================
\begin{multicols}{2}
\small
\begin{description}[leftmargin=0pt,itemsep=1.5pt]
\item[ABOP / Tersoff] analytical bond-order potential: bond strength depends on the local environment. \cmd{tersoff/zbl} adds a screened-Coulomb (ZBL) wall at short range.
\item[hybrid/overlay] several pair styles summed for the same atom pairs; here host ABOP + He tables.
\item[Pair table] potential given as $E(r)$, $F(r)$ points; LAMMPS interpolates (\cmd{linear}, $N$ points).
\item[$E_\mathrm{bulk}$] energy of the perfect relaxed cell, same box as every defect cell.
\item[Solution energy $E_\mathrm{sol}$] cost of putting one He from vacuum into the perfect lattice.
\item[Formation energy] energy of a defect relative to reference reservoirs (bulk W/WC, He). In WC needs $\mu_\mathrm W,\mu_\mathrm C$.
\item[Binding energy $\Eb$] energy released when two defects combine; positive = bound.
\item[Interaction energy $E_\mathrm{int}$] pair energy relative to two isolated He; positive = repulsive.
\item[Trap] defect that lowers He energy (vacancy, vacancy cluster).
\item[Capacity] largest $n$ with $\Eb(\He\to\He_{n-1}X)>0$.
\item[Trap mutation] He pressure pushes a host atom off its site, making a Frenkel pair; detected by $n_\mathrm{far}>0$.
\item[Channel / trigonal-prismatic site] empty prism between W layers in WC (one per C), stacked along $c$; lowest-energy interstitial He site.
\item[$V_\mathrm W$, $V_\mathrm C$] W, C vacancy; $(V_\mathrm W)_2$ c-axis or in-plane divacancy; $V_\mathrm WV_\mathrm C$ mixed.
\item[Tetrahedral / octahedral (bcc)] interstitial sites in W; He prefers tetrahedral.
\item[SIA] self-interstitial atom (in W the $\langle111\rangle$ dumbbell/crowdion).
\item[Constant-volume convention] defect cells keep the bulk box; standard for binding energies (JW).
\item[CG] conjugate-gradient minimisation; \textbf{line search} picks the step along each direction.
\item[ftol / etol] force (2-norm, eV/\AA) and relative energy stopping criteria.
\item[Anneal / quench] heat at $T$ to cross barriers, then cool to $\sim$1\,K before CG.
\item[Berendsen thermostat] rescales velocities toward a target $T$ with time constant $\tau$; good for searching, not for sampling.
\item[Configuration search] finding the lowest-energy arrangement of He (and host) among many local minima.
\item[Trial] one independent random He placement; \textbf{cycle} 1--4 = relaxation as placed / after 300 / 614.6 / 900\,K.
\item[hits] relaxations reaching the reported minimum within 1\,meV.
\item[Intact] no host atom moved $>1$\,\AA.
\item[$k_\mathrm{in}$, core] He inside the sphere (farthest site $+2.2$\,\AA); fewer than $n$ = escaped.
\item[$d_\mathrm{max}$, $n_\mathrm{far}$] largest host displacement; host atoms moved $>1$\,\AA.
\item[$R_g$] He radius of gyration; jump vs start = He spread out.
\item[Minimum image] shortest vector between two points under periodic boundaries.
\item[Restart file] binary snapshot of box, atoms and settings (\cmd{wc\_bulk0.restart}).
\item[Compute currency] (29 Sep 2021) computes are only valid on the timestep they were last evaluated.
\item[\cmd{loop geom}] velocities generated from positions, independent of IDs and MPI ranks.
\item[Migration energy $E_\mathrm m$] barrier for a jump; \textbf{detrapping} $E_\mathrm{dt}=\Eb+E_\mathrm m$.
\item[NEB] nudged elastic band: finds the minimum-energy path and barrier between two minima.
\item[DFT column] \cmd{YES} must, \cmd{rec} recommended, \cmd{check} compare with literature, \cmd{no} not needed.
\item[MAE] mean absolute error against the JW reference values.
\item[JW 2013] Juslin \& Wirth, J.\ Nucl.\ Mater.\ 432 (2013) 61 -- the benchmark.
\item[Chemical potential $\mu_s$] energy per atom of the reservoir that exchanges species $s$ with the crystal; sets formation energies.
\item[W-rich / C-rich limit] WC in equilibrium with bcc W (or W2C) / with graphite: the two ends of the $\mu$ window.
\item[Formation enthalpy $\Delta H_f$] energy of WC relative to bcc W + graphite; the width of the $\mu$ window.
\item[Convex hull / coexistence] the set of stable phases; at a two-phase boundary both phases fix $\mu$.
\item[Schottky pair] one $V_\mathrm W$ + one $V_\mathrm C$: removes a whole formula unit, so independent of $\mu$.
\item[Frenkel pair] vacancy + interstitial of the same species.
\item[Antisite] an atom on the other species' site ($W_\mathrm C$, $C_\mathrm W$).
\item[Dumbbell] two atoms sharing one lattice site, split along a direction.
\item[Site fraction $c_d$] defects per formula unit at equilibrium (dilute: $\ll1$).
\item[50/50 (stoichiometric) $\mu$] the $\mu_\mathrm C$ at which equilibrium defects keep C/W = 1; clamped to the window if impossible.
\item[Mid-window] the midpoint of the $\mu_\mathrm C$ window, a common convention.
\item[WC$_{1-y}$] C-deficient WC; beyond the W-rich limit it is a non-equilibrium (e.g.\ sputter-depleted) state.
\item[Reference phase] crystal that fixes a chemical potential, computed with the same potential.
\end{description}
\end{multicols}
